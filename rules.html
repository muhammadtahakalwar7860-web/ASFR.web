Server Guidelines

Rules & Regulations

Read and understand our rules before joining. Ignorance is not an excuse.

👥
Community Conduct Respect • Integrity • Fairness
1
Respect Everyone
Treat all members with respect. No harassment, bullying, or toxic behavior will be tolerated.
2
No Hate Speech
Racism, slurs, discrimination, or offensive language will result in instant punishment.
3
No Toxic Behavior
Trolling, provoking arguments, or intentionally disrupting the community is not allowed.
4
No Drama or Instigating
Avoid unnecessary arguments or conflict creation.
5
No Impersonation
Do not impersonate staff members, content creators, or other community members.
6
Protect Personal Information
Do not share your own or anyone else's personal information. Doxxing or threats result in an immediate permanent ban.
📋
Server Guidelines Discord Server Rules
1
No Spamming
Do not spam messages, emojis, GIFs, reactions, mentions, or media. Keep conversations readable.
2
Keep Content Appropriate
No NSFW, graphic, or disturbing content of any kind.
3
No Advertising
Do not promote other servers, socials, or links without permission — not even in DMs. If we receive a report, you can be banned.
4
No Scams or Malicious Links
Posting scam or phishing links results in an immediate ban.
5
Use Channels Properly
Stay on-topic and use the correct designated channels for your discussions.
6
English Only
Please communicate in English unless a channel specifically allows other languages.
7
Appropriate Usernames & Profiles
Usernames, nicknames, profile pictures, and statuses must remain appropriate and inoffensive.
8
Follow Discord's Terms of Service
All members must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times.
🎙️
Voice Chat Voice Channel Conduct
1
No Mic Spam or Earrape
Keep voice chat clean and respectful at all times.
2
No Soundboards or Noise Abuse
Excessive soundboard usage or intentional noise disruption is prohibited.
🎮
Roleplay Rules In-Game Roleplay Standards
1
No Fail RP
Unrealistic actions or ignoring roleplay situations is not allowed.
2
No Random Deathmatch (RDM)
Killing another player without a valid roleplay reason will result in punishment.
3
No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)
Intentionally running players over with vehicles is prohibited.
4
Value Your Life (VYL)
Act realistically in dangerous situations. Your character values their life.
5
No Trolling or Disrupting RP
Do not ruin active scenes, events, or other players' roleplay experiences.
⚖️
Staff & Administration Authority & Appeals
1
Respect & Follow Staff Instructions
Arguing with staff decisions publicly may lead to additional punishment. Use tickets for concerns.
2
Staff Decisions Are Final
If you believe a mistake was made, respectfully appeal through the proper channels (support tickets).
🚫
Exploits & Cheats Zero Tolerance Policy
1
No Exploiting or Glitch Abuse
The use, promotion, or distribution of exploits, cheats, bugs, or unauthorized software results in a permanent ban.
⚠️ Violations & Punishments

Violations may result in escalating consequences based on severity:

Warning → Timeout / Mute → Kick → Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban
Ban-Worthy Offenses (Immediate):
Exploiting / Hacking Hate Speech NSFW Content Scamming Severe Harassment Ban Evasion
📜 Rules apply to both Discord & Roblox
🧠 Ignorance of rules is not an excuse
💡 Not every situation can be covered by these rules. Use common sense and contribute to a friendly and professional community.
📢 Make sure to read all rules before joining sessions!
